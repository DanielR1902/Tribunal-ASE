Arguments of Record

STAGE 01 · ADVOCACY
Crown Advocate
PROSECUTION

The accused, sworn to the office of Lord Commander, struck down the seated sovereign in open court. The oath of fealty admits no private exception: a subject may not appoint himself the arbiter of when allegiance lapses. However grave the sovereign's conduct, the Realm maintained standing organs of impeachment and trial, and those organs were never petitioned.

Prayer: conviction for regicide and breach of sworn duty; the deterrent interest of the Realm is paramount.

Counsel for the Accused
DEFENCE

The act followed the mass immolation of a surrendered civilian population and an announced intention to continue. No tribunal was available; no lesser measure could have arrested the harm within the hours that remained. The defence pleads necessity and the defence of others, both recognised where the peril is imminent, unlawful, and otherwise unavoidable.

Prayer: acquittal; alternatively, mitigation to exile in lieu of capital sentence.

In re The Realm v. Jon Snow — Judgment of the Tribunal
ONE PROMPT · {{ engineLabel }} · 3 doctrines synthesised

The accused is charged with regicide and breach of a sworn commission. The material facts are not in dispute: the sovereign, having accepted the surrender of a defended city, ordered and oversaw the destruction of its civilian population, and declared the campaign unfinished. The accused, holding no judicial office, killed the sovereign in private audience some hours later.

{{ j.synthesis }}

Reconciling the three approaches, the tribunal holds that the prohibition on regicide is not absolute where the sovereign has become the instrument of ongoing mass killing and no institution of the Realm remains capable of restraining him within the time available. The doctrine of necessity applies; the institutional objection is answered not by denying its force but by confining this holding to circumstances where every lawful organ has demonstrably failed.

DISPOSITION Acquitted on grounds of extreme necessity — conditional exile entered
